MARKETCRAFT / 02

01 / Market positioning

Understand demand before buying reach.

MARKETCRAFT / 03

Audience and service context

Focus on service reliability, not demographic stereotypes.

Everyday riders

Depend on their bicycle for routine transport; prioritize predictable downtime.

Recreational riders

Plan maintenance around weekend outings; value clear service options.

Validation

Recruit riders at repair shops and community cycling events; compare actual booking behavior with stated preferences.

MARKETCRAFT / 04

Growth objective

A bounded pilot with an observable outcome.

Pilot target

Over 12 weeks, achieve 90 completed first-time services attributable to the pilot, within a $2,700 acquisition budget.

Guardrails

Maintain at least 90% on-time completion and avoid exceeding available service slots.

Decision

Expand only after validating incremental demand and contribution margin. All targets are illustrative.

MARKETCRAFT / 05

Measurement framework

Separate acquisition from operational delivery.

Primary measure

Completed first-time services attributed to the pilot; deduplicate using service IDs.

Efficiency

Acquisition spend divided by completed first-time services; target ceiling $30.

Quality

On-time completions divided by completed services; review weekly with the service manager.

Attribution

Use tagged links and booking records; compare with a holdout where feasible.

MARKETCRAFT / 06

Alternative landscape

Independent repair shops and self-service alternatives.

Established workshops

Trusted technicians; existing customer relationships.

Limitations

Appointment uncertainty and inconsistent digital updates may create friction.

Self-service alternatives

Low labor cost and immediate access for skilled riders.

Trade-offs

Tools, expertise and safety checks may be unavailable to less experienced riders.

MARKETCRAFT / 08

Positioning narrative

Make maintenance easier to plan.

Customer job

Keep a bicycle dependable without losing an unpredictable amount of time.

Promise

Book a service window, approve an estimate and receive progress updates.

Proof to develop

Measure estimate accuracy, completion reliability and repeat bookings before making public performance claims.

MARKETCRAFT / 09

02 / Customer intelligence

Design for the decisions riders actually make.

MARKETCRAFT / 10

Empathy and behavior hypotheses

Research prompts, not interview findings.

Thinking

Will the repair cost more than expected? Can I get my bicycle back before work?

Seeing

Repair tutorials, workshop reviews and community recommendations.

Doing

Checking appointment availability and comparing service menus.

Feeling

Uncertain about technical diagnosis; relieved by a clear estimate.

MARKETCRAFT / 11

Customer profile / Alex

Fictional behavioral archetype: the time-constrained rider.

Context

Uses a bicycle for local errands and commuting; schedules maintenance around work.

Needs

Clear appointment window; approval before extra work.

Goals

Avoid unexpected downtime; keep transport dependable.

Barriers

Unclear prices, inconvenient drop-off and uncertainty about repair duration.

Interests

Local cycling routes and practical maintenance tips.

Research plan

Test this archetype with actual riders before treating it as a segment.

MARKETCRAFT / 12

03 / Customer journey

Reduce uncertainty at every handoff.

MARKETCRAFT / 13

Journey / discovery to booking

Connect customer actions with measurable service improvements.

Discover

Local search or cycling group; wants a nearby option. Measure qualified visits.

Evaluate

Service menu and estimate ranges; wants cost clarity. Measure estimate-to-booking conversion.

Book

Appointment calendar; wants a suitable slot. Measure booking completion.

Service

Drop-off and progress updates; wants certainty. Measure on-time completion.

MARKETCRAFT / 14

Retention friction

A service reminder should be useful, not intrusive.

Hypothesis

Riders may ignore generic reminders that do not reflect their actual service history.

Intervention

Offer an optional reminder based on the completed service category; let riders choose timing and unsubscribe.

Experiment

Compare a relevant reminder with no reminder among consenting customers. Track repeat completed services and opt-outs.

MARKETCRAFT / 15

04 / Content and activation

Turn strategy into testable communication.

MARKETCRAFT / 16

Content system / objectives

A lifecycle plan aligned with the customer job.

Discover

Explain common maintenance symptoms through local search content.

Evaluate

Show service categories, estimate ranges and appointment availability.

Book

Provide a clear booking action and cancellation policy.

Return

Share relevant maintenance guidance after completed service.

MARKETCRAFT / 17

Content system / experience

Design messages around friction, not just impressions.

Discover

Avoid fear-based messaging; explain when a check is useful.

Evaluate

Reduce uncertainty with clear inclusions and estimate approval.

Book

Minimize form fields; show the next available appointment.

Return

Use optional reminders and avoid unnecessary personal data.

Community / evaluation

Compare short and detailed intake forms; monitor cancellations.
